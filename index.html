Why Green Hydrogen Matters

Green hydrogen is the only scalable zero-carbon fuel capable of decarbonizing hard-to-abate sectors — steel, chemicals, heavy transport, shipping, and aviation. Unlike batteries, hydrogen enables long-duration seasonal energy storage and can be transported globally via existing infrastructure.

Current Electrolysis Technologies

💧 PEM Electrolysis

Efficiency 65–80%, rapid response, perfect for variable renewables. Uses proton exchange membrane for high-purity output.

⚡ Alkaline Electrolysis

Efficiency 60–75%, mature technology, cost-effective at scale. Best suited for stable baseload renewable supply.

🔥 SOEC (High-Temperature)

Efficiency 85–90%, leverages waste heat for superior energy economics. Ideal for industrial co-location.

🧪 AEM Electrolysis

Emerging technology — no precious metals required, lower cost potential. Combines advantages of PEM and Alkaline.

Note: the technologies above are provided as general industry context. Aaru Green Hydrogen's own work focuses specifically on alkaline water electrolysis — including process simulation, commercial stack design, and performance modeling.
